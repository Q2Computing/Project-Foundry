The sections

A · Sep 28 to Oct 04

Certificates into the catalog: Sophia's IEEE 754 components and the proven cell basis

The automation runs the RTL to GDSII pipeline on each component and marks it complete only when every automation test and the external conformance suites pass. The human verifiers sign off against the specification, and the final signoff authorizes any on-chain step. Certificates and their timings land in the portal's analysis records, which is also the measured cost record, and the public catalog is re-exported from those records. September 28: the proven standard-cell basis grew from 4 cells to 65, every drive strength the PDK provides for 23 families, each proved over its full input space; the catalog lists 124 blocks.

B · Sep 29 to Oct 03

FPGA enumeration at Generator: unary FP32 against SoftFloat

Square root, conversions, rounding to integral, and classification each take one 32-bit input, so the whole space of 4.3 billion cases is enumerated on the board and compared, by digest, with Berkeley SoftFloat. A complete run is a level-1 proof that the block refines IEEE 754, with SoftFloat and the bitstream toolchain named as what it trusts. The rounding decision and the special cases of the wide operations are enumerated the same week.

F · Oct 05 to Oct 23

FP32 add and multiply refine IEEE 754 by the three stages

The exact stage composes from proven adders; the rounding stage and the special cases are level-1 enumerations from week one; the Lean theorem that exact-then-correctly-rounded equals correctly-rounded is the level-4 glue. The certificate reads: level 4, composed from proven children, refines IEEE 754, trusts SoftFloat and the solver.

After the calendar

  1. Proof-carrying trust: certificate replay. The checker emits terms a verified checker replays, so no solver is trusted on its word. Roadmap, no date.
  2. Tape-out on a sky130 shuttle. On ChipFoundry chipIgnite shuttle CI2612: commitment November 4, tape-out December 7, 2026, parts delivered May 25, 2027; $14,950 for up to 15 mm² of design area and 100 packaged parts. Every block on the die carries a certificate, the top-level certificate carries the hash of the GDS sent to the foundry, and physical signoff at the declared clock is recorded as the manufacturability verdict.
  3. Silicon attestation, first-time-right test. The returned part runs the same SoftFloat and Sail reference vectors under a pass criterion fixed before it returns. A pass closes the attestation row with a dated record. A failure is published with its cause: custody, signoff, or process.
  4. Funding gate. The first-time-right part is the feasibility evidence for a Phase II application. The vehicle depends on the agency: NSF funds Phase I before Phase II; Direct-to-Phase II paths exist at NIH and at Department of Defense components. The solicitation is read before it is treated as pursuable.
  5. Public release of the portal. After March 1, 2027.
