Every block with a passing proof of record, exported from the portal's analysis table on 2026-09-28.

A row is a block whose proof artifact passed at the level shown. The hash is the content address of that artifact, the proof of record a certificate would carry. Blocks are listed by the highest level they reach; a composed block's certificate rests on its children's, which are also here. Human sign-off is recorded where a person has verified the block in the portal; the proof stands on its own without it, and the sign-off is the second and third of the three checks that precede any chain transaction.
