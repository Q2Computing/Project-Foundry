The Proof Economy

A proof of a circuit is a certificate. Certificates license into larger proofs and into manufactured silicon, at a floor no competitor can match, settled on Arbitrum Stylus.

Verification is shared, not repeated. A block is proved once, in public, and every system built on it inherits the proof. This site is the public instance of that economy: the levels of proof every certificate is graded by, the two licenses, the three contracts, and the loop that beats foundry cells and proves it. The scientific record behind every number is on the research page.

The levels of proof

Every certificate names the level at which its block is proved. The ladder runs from the strongest bounded proof to the one that scales without bound. Composition is the top and it is closed: a composed block is a part for the next composition.

The economy

Reference: the commons

Cost recovery, never rent

Foundry PDK primitives are free to reference forever. Every other certificate recovers only its listing gas, paid by the abstractions that directly use it, capped at that gas, then it is free. Whoever beats a block pays its listing cost.

Manufacturing: the designer's product

$100 per mask set, SoC down

The right to put a design into a fabricated SoC. The design goes only to a designer-named foundry, which must prove it received the committed package before it may consume a unit. The licensee never downloads the file. The floor is walked down as adoption grows, toward what a proof physically costs to produce and settle.

The protocol takes zero in both licenses. Fifty-eight machine-checked theorems back the economics; see the research page.

Settlement on Arbitrum Stylus

The point of a win is not a screenshot, it is a durable, verifiable record. Each verified improvement is committed as a hash for a Stylus contract on Arbitrum to record. Anyone holding the netlist can recompute the hash and check it; no one else can read the design

record(bytes32 artifact_hash, uint16 label, uint16 context)
// best in-band win: EDP +22% vs buf_16, same area
record(0xbbe5a7f1...f8f7c, 2200, 16)
//         hash of the design bundle   basis points   drive strength

label is basis points of EDP improvement, context is the part's drive strength. The hash is the artifact; the netlist never has to be published. The contract is written in Rust for Arbitrum Stylus and passes cargo stylus check. Status: not deployed. anchor.py prepares this record() call, it does not submit it (that needs a funded key)
