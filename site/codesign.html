The dopant knob g runs between two real sky130 endpoints, both fabricable today: g=0 is nfet_svt + pfet_hvt (highest Vt, low leakage, slower); g=1 is nfet_lvt + pfet_svt (lowest Vt available, faster, leakier). sky130 ships nfet svt/lvt and pfet svt/hvt, so those two combos bound a mask set. A value in between is fabricable today as a mask blend: k of the m fingers on one Vt mask, the rest on the other, in parallel. No new implant, no hypothesis; the 50/50 blend is a measured point on the in-band chart. Measured on the unit inverter, the two speed knobs are asymmetric: nfet svt→lvt buys −9.7% on the fall edge for 3× leakage (nearly free), pfet hvt→svt buys −23% on the rise edge for 121× leakage (the expensive knob). That asymmetry is why context, not a fixed recipe, should pick the dopant. The model is calibrated to SPICE and matched it within ~6% at a confirmed endpoint (details).
