buf_16 in five sky130 libraries at 250 fF

This page is the measure step of the cell differentiation service on the front page: the foundry's own buf_16 from each of sky130's five standard-cell libraries, measured on one bench.

One ngspice run, typical corner, 1.8 V, 50 ps input slew, every cell driving the same 250 fF load. The device lists come from the vendored foundry netlists; the traces and numbers come from this run. Reproduce with python3 analysis/differentiate.py

Each library picks its transistor flavors and widths for a different job. hd, hdll and ls pair a standard-threshold nfet with a high-threshold pfet, which leaks least; ms and hs pair a low-threshold nfet with a standard-threshold pfet, which switches faster and leaks more. hs, ms and ls sit in taller rows (3.33 µm against 2.72 µm), which is where their extra area comes from. Area is the cell's laid-out footprint from its LEF.
